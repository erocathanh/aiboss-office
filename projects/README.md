# Project Structure Standard

Every project in AIBOSS Office follows a standardized directory structure:

## Core Governance Files
- `CHANNEL.md`: Channel log for communications between Owner, PM, and agents.
- `PROJECT.md`: Project charter, objectives, boundaries, and scope.
- `DECISIONS.md`: Log of accepted and signed decisions.
- `DEFERRED-DECISIONS.md`: Log of decisions deferred for future consideration.
- `PROGRESS.md`: Periodic execution marks and status reports.

## Working Directories
- `inputs/`: Inbound assets, briefs, and client requirements.
- `wip/`: Work-in-progress artifacts.
- `wip/submissions/`: Deliverables submitted for review and acceptance.
- `outputs/`: Final accepted deliverables ready for deployment or delivery.
- `references/`: Reference material, documentation, and external research.
- `templates/`: Project-specific templates and boilerplates.
- `archive/`: Deprecated or historical versions.
- `research/`: Research notes, market analysis, and audits.
- `briefs/`: Operational briefs for individual seats.
- `sessions/`: Logs of agent execution sessions.
- `private/`: Sensitive information excluded from version control.
